# DStarix Internship Guide - Sample Questions

Here is a list of sample questions you can ask the chatbot to test its Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) capabilities based on the DStarix Internship Rule Book:

### 🕒 Duration & Schedule
1. **What is the duration of the internship?**
2. **What are the working days and flexible hours guidelines?**
3. **Is there a fixed daily login schedule?**

### 📚 Structure & Phases
4. **What is the structure of the internship?**
5. **What happens during Phase 1 (Learning Phase)?**
6. **What happens during Phase 2 (Project Phase)?**

### 💻 GitHub & AI Tools
7. **What are the guidelines for using GitHub and version control?**
8. **Can I use AI tools like ChatGPT or Claude?**
9. **What are the requirements if I use AI-generated code?**

### ✉️ Communication & Support
10. **Who should I contact for queries and what is the email address?**
11. **What details should be included when contacting the mentor?**

### 📝 Submission & Deadlines
12. **What are the guidelines for task submissions?**
13. **Does a late submission affect my evaluation?**

### 🎓 Evaluation & Completion
14. **How is the internship performance evaluated?**
15. **Will I receive an Internship Completion Certificate?**
